OUR STORY

A small studio, a life-long
love affair with flowers.

Floerella began in 2016 with a single wooden bench, a pair of Japanese shears and
a stubborn belief that a bouquet can change a whole day.

Our Mission

To make luxury florals feel personal — sourced from growers we know by first name, arranged by hand, and delivered with a written note. We work in small batches so nothing is ever mass-produced.
